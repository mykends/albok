# English Grammar Basics

This short sample book shows how Albok splits an imported file into sections. Each `##` heading below becomes one section you can follow in class.

## 1.1 Present Simple and Present Continuous

Use the **present simple** for habits, routines and facts. Use the **present continuous** for things happening now or around now.

- She *walks* to school every day. (habit)
- Water *boils* at 100 °C. (fact)
- She *is walking* to school right now. (happening now)

> Words like *always*, *usually* and *never* usually go with the present simple; *now*, *at the moment* and *this week* with the present continuous.

### Try it

Put the verb in the correct form: "Listen! Someone ___ (knock) at the door."

## 1.2 Past Simple and Present Perfect

Use the **past simple** for finished actions at a known time in the past. Use the **present perfect** for past actions connected to now, when the exact time is not important.

1. I *visited* London in 2023. (finished, time given)
2. I *have visited* London twice. (experience, no time given)
3. She *has just finished* her homework. (recent, result now)

### Try it

Choose the correct form: "We ___ (see / have seen) that film last weekend."

## 1.3 Conditionals

Conditional sentences talk about a condition and its result.

| Type | Use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Zero | general truths | If you heat ice, it melts. |
| First | real future possibility | If it rains, we will stay inside. |
| Second | unreal present or future | If I had more time, I would learn Japanese. |
| Third | unreal past | If she had studied, she would have passed. |

## 1.4 Useful Vocabulary: School Life

- **timetable**: the list of lessons and when they happen
- **assignment**: a piece of work given by a teacher
- **deadline**: the date by which work must be handed in
- **revise**: to study again before a test

### Try it

Write three sentences about your school week using at least two of the words above.
